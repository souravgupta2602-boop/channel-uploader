Channel Uploader — Privacy Policy & Terms

Last updated: 27 October 2026

1. What this app is

Channel Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the Popcorn Index, Operational Universe, Ghost Atlas, Miles & Memories and Baby Bear Toons channels. It publishes short videos the owner made to the owner's own accounts on YouTube, Instagram and TikTok, with the caption and post time the owner chose. It is not offered to the public and no one else can sign in to it.

2. Privacy Policy

What it uses. The app signs in with each platform's official login (Google / YouTube Data API, Instagram API with Instagram Login, TikTok Login Kit) only for the owner's own accounts. On TikTok it uses the user.info.basic scope to show which account is connected, and video.upload / video.publish to send the owner's video to that account.

What it stores. Sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer. Videos are kept on the owner's computer and, for Instagram, in a private storage bucket that deletes them automatically within one day.

What it never does. It does not collect, store, sell or share any data about other people, viewers, followers or commenters, and it does not read anyone's messages. No data is sent anywhere except to the platform the video is being posted to.

Removing access. Access can be removed at any time: Google at myaccount.google.com/permissions, TikTok under Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions, Instagram under Settings → Apps and websites.

3. Terms of Service

The app is provided as is, for the channel owner's personal use only. The owner is responsible for the videos it posts and for following the YouTube Terms of Service, the Instagram Terms of Use and the TikTok Terms of Service and Community Guidelines. Use of YouTube API Services is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and Google's Privacy Policy.
