Channel Uploader — Privacy Policy & Terms

Last updated: 24 October 2026 · Google Cloud app name: Popcorn Index

1. What this app is

A private tool used only by the owner of the Popcorn Index, Operational Universe, Ghost Atlas, Miles & Memories and Baby Bear Toons YouTube channels. It uploads videos the owner made to the owner's own channels and sets their titles, descriptions, thumbnails and publish times. It is not offered to the public.

2. What data it uses

The app signs in with Google (YouTube Data API) only to manage the owner's own channels. It reads the channel's list of uploaded and scheduled videos so it does not schedule two videos at the same time, and it uploads new videos, thumbnails and captions.

3. What it stores

The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's own computer. The app does not collect, store, sell or share any data about other people, viewers or commenters. No data is sent anywhere except to YouTube.

4. YouTube and Google

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's Privacy Policy applies to data handled by Google. You can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

5. Terms of use

The app is provided as is, for the channel owner's personal use only. The owner is responsible for the videos it uploads and for following YouTube's policies.
