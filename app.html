The Channel Uploader helper isn't running on this computer.

Start it with python tiktok_app.py, then reload this page. Your TikTok tokens and videos never leave your computer.

1. Connect your TikTok account

You'll be sent to TikTok to log in and approve. We only see your public profile name and picture (user.info.basic) and post videos you choose (video.upload, video.publish).
